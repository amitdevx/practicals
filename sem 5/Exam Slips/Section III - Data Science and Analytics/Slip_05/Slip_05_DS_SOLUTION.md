## Question 3: Oral / Viva Questions & Answers [5 Marks]

### Q1. What is Label Encoding?
**Answer:** It is a technique used to convert categorical variables into numerical values so that they can be processed by machine learning algorithms.

### Q2. How does KNN choose the class for a new point?
**Answer:** It computes the distance between the new point and all training points, selects the K nearest neighbors, and assigns the class that is most frequent among those neighbors.

### Q3. What is the difference between a Pie Chart and a Venn Diagram?
**Answer:** A Pie Chart shows proportions of a whole, while a Venn Diagram visualizes overlapping relationships and intersections among different sets.
